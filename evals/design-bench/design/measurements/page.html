How a batch becomes a claim

Measurements are run in discrete batches to ensure stable variables. A batch specifies the target models, the baseline tools, the rendering pipeline configurations, and the exact metric formulation applied to the outputs.

When a batch completes, its outputs are hashed to a definition file, ensuring that the metric scores shown here are immutably tied to the exact prompts and code that generated them. Claims in the gallery link back to these definition hashes to provide reproducible proof.
