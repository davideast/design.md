Our tea is a record of travel. Every year, we return to the high-altitude gardens of Taiwan to source oolongs that capture the specific clarity of the mountain air. From there, we journey to the highlands of Kenya, where the mineral-rich soil produces black teas of singular depth and warmth. These trips are not merely for procurement; we visit these farms as guests, sitting for hours over shared cups to ensure every leaf reflects the integrity of the land and the hands that harvest it.
