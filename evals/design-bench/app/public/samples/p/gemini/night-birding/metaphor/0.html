Guide to Listening The Urban Filter

To identify birds by sound in the city, you must mentally subtract the machine:

1. THE BASE (NOISE) Low-frequency rumble of the expressway or subway vents.
2. THE SIGNAL The "Peent"—a sharp, nasal electric buzz cutting through the hum.
3. THE PHYSICAL The "Whoosh" of air through primary feathers during a territorial dive.
In Flight Mechanics

Thick neck tucked tightly against shoulders; feet barely extending beyond the short, square tail.

Alert

Crepuscular activity peak. High probability of detection at shoreline interface.

Context: Often found near waterfront construction sites or park ponds.
