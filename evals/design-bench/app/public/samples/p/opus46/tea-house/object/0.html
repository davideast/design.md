Every spring and autumn, our owner Lin travels to the high mountain farms of Chiayi County in Taiwan and the rolling estates of Nandi Hills in Kenya. These aren't buying trips in any corporate sense — they're visits to farmers she's known for over a decade. In Taiwan, she cups the new flush directly at the processing shed, choosing lots by hand. In Kenya, she walks the rows at altitude, watching how the purple-leaf cultivar responds to the season's rains. What arrives at Kettle & Leaf has been carried by hand, packed in foil-lined craft bags, and is never more than eight weeks from the bush.
