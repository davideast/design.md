Georgetown Waterfront Track

Where the River Keeps the Pace

The Georgetown Waterfront Track sits just above the Potomac, a 400-meter oval where the city's diplomats, students, and dawn regulars share lanes in quiet rotation. The surface is newer than most — a rubberized compound laid in 2016 — but the rhythm is old: the same river light each morning, the same towpath breeze pulling at the back straight. It is the kind of track that earns loyalty through consistency, not spectacle.

Anacostia Park Track

The East Side's Quiet Engine

Anacostia's track is older and less celebrated, but its regulars would not trade it. Set within the broad green expanse of Anacostia Park, the oval is a 440-yard cinder loop that still carries the texture of a different era. Morning runners here are neighborhood fixtures — coaches who trained on this same surface decades ago, families walking the outer lane, sprinters working intervals in lane one.
