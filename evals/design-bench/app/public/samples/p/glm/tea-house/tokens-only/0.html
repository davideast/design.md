03 /From the Owner

Mei-Lin Cho
on sourcing.

Twice a year I fly to Taiwan and ride the slow train down to Beipu, where Mr. Hwang's family has finished Oriental Beauty on the same bamboo trays for four generations. We taste the spring and winter lots across his kitchen table, and I bring back what I can carry in a hard-shell case.

In alternate autumns I take the road north from Nairobi into the Nyeri highlands, where the Kangaita cooperative farms small plots on red volcanic soil at 1,900 metres. The farmers cup with me on the drying patio, and we settle the season's price over a shared thermos before I leave.

Every leaf in this shop has a face and a name attached to it. That is the only way I know how to run a tea house.
