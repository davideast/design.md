Guide to Listening: The Urban Filter

To identify birds by sound in the city, you must mentally subtract the machine:

  1. 01 The Base

    Ignore the low-frequency rumble of the expressway or subway vents.

  2. 02 The Signal

    Focus on the “Peent” — a sharp, nasal electric buzz cutting through the hum.

  3. 03 The Physical

    Listen for the “Whoosh” of air through primary feathers during a territorial dive.
