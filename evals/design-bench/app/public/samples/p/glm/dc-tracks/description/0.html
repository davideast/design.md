Plate 01 Georgetown · 38.9044° N
Georgetown Waterfront Track

Where the river keeps time, and the rowers set the pace.

Built into the bank of the Potomac, this four-lane oval is the city's most photographed and least forgiving. Crew shells slide past on the water while early risers trace its tight curves in the half-light, the surface still cool from the night.

It is short, it is crowded, and it is loved — a track that asks you to mind your neighbors and keep your shoulders loose.

Plate 02 Anacostia · 38.8683° N
Anacostia Park Track

Eight lanes wide, and quiet until the high schools arrive.

On the east bank of the Anacostia, this eight-lane track is the city's most generous — broad, well-kept, and ringed by elms that throw long shadows across lane one in the late afternoon.

It belongs, by midweek, to the cross-country teams of the surrounding high schools; by Saturday morning, to the slow and the solitary, who appreciate a track that does not ask them to hurry.

Distance 400 m
Surface Polyurethane
