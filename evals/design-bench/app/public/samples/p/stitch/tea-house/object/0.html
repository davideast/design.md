LINER NOTES

Our sourcing is a matter of mileage and patience. Each spring, we travel from the mist-shrouded peaks of Alishan, Taiwan, to the sun-drenched gardens of Kenya, sitting with growers to select the season’s most singular harvests. We don’t just buy tea; we document the weather, the soil, and the hands that pluck every leaf.
