Our Sourcing

From Mountain to Kettle

Every leaf we serve is the result of direct relationships with farmers. From the high-elevation gardens hidden in the misty peaks of Taiwan to the innovative smallholder farms cultivating rare varieties in Kenya, our sourcing trips are focused entirely on quality, sustainability, and fair trade. We bring the world's most exceptional, unblended teas directly to your cup.
