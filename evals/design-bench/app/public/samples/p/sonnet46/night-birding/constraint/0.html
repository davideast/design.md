Guide to Listening: The Urban Filter INK-04
To identify birds by sound in the city, you must mentally subtract the machine:
1 The Base Ignore the low-frequency rumble of the expressway or subway vents.
2 The Signal Focus on the "Peent"—a sharp, nasal electric buzz cutting through the hum.
3 Physical Listen for the "Whoosh" of air through primary feathers during a territorial dive.
