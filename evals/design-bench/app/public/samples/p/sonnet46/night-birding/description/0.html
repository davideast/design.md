§ Guide to Listening: The Urban Filter

To identify birds by sound in the city, you must mentally subtract the machine:

01 The Base Ignore the low-frequency rumble of the expressway or subway vents.
02 The Signal Focus on the "Peent"—a sharp, nasal electric buzz cutting through the hum.
03 Physical Listen for the "Whoosh" of air through primary feathers during a territorial dive.
