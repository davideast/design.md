From the source

Owner Mei-Lin Huang travels twice a year to find what ends up in your cup. Each spring she visits the high-mountain gardens of Taiwan's Ali Shan and Sun Moon Lake, sitting with the families who have tended these rows for three generations. Each autumn she travels to the coastal smallholdings near Kenya's Lamu island, where a small group of growers have begun producing white and green teas of quiet distinction. These are not buying trips in the transactional sense — they are long friendships built cup by cup, and what arrives here is what those friendships allow.
