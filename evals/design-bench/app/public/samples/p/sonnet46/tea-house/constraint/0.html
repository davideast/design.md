From the source

Owner Mei-Lin Huang travels twice a year to find what ends up in your cup. Each spring she visits the high-mountain gardens of Taiwan — Ali Shan and Sun Moon Lake — sitting with the families who have cultivated these slopes for three generations. Each autumn she travels to the coastal smallholdings near Lamu, Kenya, where a small community of growers has begun producing white and green teas of quiet distinction. These are friendships built cup by cup across many years. What arrives here is what those friendships allow.
