Today's brewed teas

Alishan High Mountain
Ali Shan, Taiwan
Oolong
Creamy orchid sweetness with a sustained floral finish. Grown at 1,400 metres in mist-shrouded gardens, this oolong is picked by hand in early spring when the air still carries winter's chill.
Lamu Island White
Coast Province, Kenya
White Tea
Delicate and honeyed, with a clean melon note and a quiet mineral finish. One of the few white teas produced on the Kenyan coast, picked at first light and sun-dried on woven mats.
Ruby 18
Sun Moon Lake, Taiwan
Black Tea
Warm cinnamon and natural wintergreen, with a smooth cocoa close and no astringency. A cultivar unique to Taiwan's Sun Moon Lake, developed over decades for its singular softness.

From the source

Owner Mei-Lin Huang travels twice a year to find what ends up in your cup. Each spring she visits the high-mountain gardens of Taiwan — Ali Shan and Sun Moon Lake — sitting with the families who have cultivated these slopes for three generations. The conversations are long, the meals shared, and the trust built slowly across many visits. Each autumn she travels to the coastal smallholdings near Lamu, Kenya, where a small community of growers has begun producing white and green teas of quiet, unassuming quality. She brings back only what she would want to drink herself, which is why the menu is short and changes with the seasons.
