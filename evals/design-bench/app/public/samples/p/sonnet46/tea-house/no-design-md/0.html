From the Source

Every tea on our menu begins with a trip. Owner Mei-Lin Huang spends two weeks each spring in the high-mountain gardens of Taiwan's Ali Shan and Sun Moon Lake, tasting alongside the families who have been growing tea for three generations. Each autumn she travels to Kenya's coastal smallholdings, where a small cluster of growers near Lamu have begun producing white and green teas that quietly rival anything from East Asia. These aren't buying trips in the ordinary sense — they're long conversations over shared meals, slow walks through wet rows of leaf, and a kind of trust built cup by cup over many years. What arrives here is what those friendships allow.
