From the source

Owner Mei-Lin Huang travels twice a year to find what ends up in your cup. Each spring she visits the high-mountain gardens of Taiwan — Ali Shan and Sun Moon Lake — sitting with the families who have cultivated these slopes for three generations. The conversations are long, the meals shared, and the trust built slowly across many visits. Each autumn she travels to the coastal smallholdings near Lamu, Kenya, where a small community of growers has begun producing white and green teas of quiet, unassuming quality. She brings back only what she would want to drink herself, which is why the menu is short and changes with the seasons.
