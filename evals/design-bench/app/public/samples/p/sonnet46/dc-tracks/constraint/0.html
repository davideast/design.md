Track No. 01  ·  Georgetown

Where the Potomac Keeps the Pace

Georgetown Waterfront's oval sits at the juncture of canal and river. The surface is fresh tartan, meticulously resurfaced in 2019 — springy underfoot, consistent across all eight lanes. The back straight faces directly into the western light at dusk.

Regulars: scullers before the water warms, Hill staffers on lunch breaks, and the solitary midnight circuit when the towpath goes quiet.

Distance 400 m
Surface Polyurethane / Tartan
Ward Ward 2
"The oval teaches you that return is not defeat — it is the whole point."

Track No. 02  ·  Anacostia

The Oldest Oval East of the River

Anacostia Park's track carries decades in its surface — rubberized asphalt that absorbs heat by ten in the morning and holds it past evening. No gate, no fee, no expected pace: the oval belongs to whoever shows up.

The Anacostia River is close enough to hear on windless nights. Reagan National's flight paths mark the laps in 737 shadow intervals — the most honest split timer in the city.

Distance ~400 m
Surface Rubberized Asphalt
Ward Ward 8
