Track No. 01  ·  Georgetown

Where the Potomac Keeps the Pace

Georgetown Waterfront's oval sits where canal meets river. At dusk the water doubles the sky so completely that a runner on the back straight feels suspended between two horizons. Fresh tartan, meticulously kept.

Regulars: scullers before the water warms, Hill staffers at noon, the solitary who come after midnight when the towpath empties and the lanes glow faint orange.

Distance 400 m
Surface Polyurethane / Tartan
Ward Ward 2
"The oval teaches you that return is not defeat — it is the whole point."

Track No. 02  ·  Anacostia

The Oldest Oval East of the River

Anacostia Park's track carries decades in its surface — rubberized asphalt that absorbs heat by ten in the morning and holds it past evening. No gate, no fee, no expected pace. The oval belongs to whoever shows up.

The Anacostia River is close enough to hear on windless nights. Reagan's flight paths mark the laps in 737 shadows — the most honest split timer in the city.

Distance ~400 m
Surface Rubberized Asphalt
Ward Ward 8
