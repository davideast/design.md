Track No. 01  ·  Georgetown

Where the Potomac Keeps the Pace

Georgetown Waterfront's oval sits where the C&O Canal meets the river, and at dusk the water doubles the sky so completely that a runner on the back straight feels momentarily suspended between two horizons. The surface is fresh tartan, meticulously maintained.

Its regulars are divided: the scullers who run before the water warms, the Hill staffers on lunch breaks, and the solitary who come after midnight when the towpath empties.

Distance 400 m
Surface Polyurethane / Tartan
Ward Ward 2
"The oval teaches you that return is not defeat — it is the whole point."

Track No. 02  ·  Anacostia

The Oldest Oval East of the River

Anacostia Park's track carries the weight of decades in its surface — rubberized asphalt that absorbs heat by ten in the morning and holds it well past evening. The infield has witnessed little league, political rallies, and the quiet Tuesday walkers who have circled here since 1987.

No gate, no entry fee, no expectation of speed. The Anacostia River is close enough to hear on windless nights, and the Reagan flight paths mark your laps in plane-shadow intervals.

Distance ~400 m
Surface Rubberized Asphalt
Ward Ward 8
