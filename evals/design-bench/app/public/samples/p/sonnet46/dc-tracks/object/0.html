Track No. 01  ·  Georgetown Waterfront

Where the Potomac Keeps the Pace

Georgetown Waterfront's oval has been the association's premier venue since the canal authority granted access in the summer of 1938. The surface — fresh tartan, relaid in 2019 — remains the finest in the district.

The timekeeper notes that wind off the river adds between two and four seconds per lap on the back straight. Members know to adjust accordingly.

Distance 400 m
Surface Polyurethane / Tartan
Ward Ward 2
Record 3:52.4 — J. Ellison, 1971

Track No. 02  ·  Anacostia Park

The Oldest Oval East of the River

The Anacostia Park track has been in continuous use since the Works Progress Administration laid its original surface in 1937. The rubberized asphalt now beneath the feet of its visitors is the fourth resurfacing; the oval itself has not moved.

Open without gate or fee, it remains the most democratic of the association's recorded venues — no credential required, no expected pace, only the enduring geometry of the lap.

Distance ~400 m
Surface Rubberized Asphalt
Ward Ward 8
Est. 1937
