Track No. 01  ·  Georgetown

Where the River Holds the Pace

Georgetown Waterfront's oval sits precisely where the C&O Canal meets the Potomac, and at dusk the water doubles the sky so completely that a runner on the back straight feels momentarily suspended between two horizons. The surface is fresh tartan, consistently well-maintained.

Its regulars are diverse: the scullers who run before the water warms, Hill staffers on lunch breaks, and the solitary midnight runners when the towpath goes quiet.

Distance 400 m
Surface Polyurethane / Tartan
Ward Ward 2
"The oval teaches you that return is not defeat — it is the whole point."

Track No. 02  ·  Anacostia

The Oldest Oval East of the River

Anacostia Park's track carries the weight of decades in its surface — rubberized asphalt that holds the morning heat well past noon. The infield has hosted little league games, community events, and the quiet persistence of the same group of elderly walkers who have come every Tuesday since 1987.

No gate, no fee, no expected speed. The Anacostia River is close enough to hear on windless evenings, and the Reagan flight paths measure your laps in 737 shadows.

Distance ~400 m
Surface Rubberized Asphalt
Ward Ward 8
