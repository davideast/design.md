Track No. 1  ·  Georgetown

Where the Potomac Keeps the Pace

Georgetown Waterfront's oval sits where the canal meets the river, and at dusk the water doubles the sky so completely that a runner moving through the back straight feels momentarily unmoored — suspended between two horizons. The surface is fresh tartan, springy underfoot, maintained with the obsessive care that only proximity to wealth permits.

Regulars here are divided: the rowers who run before the water warms, the Hill staffers on lunch breaks, and the solitary who come after midnight when the towpath empties and the lane lines glow faint orange under the sodium lamps.

Distance 400m

Track No. 2  ·  Anacostia

The Oldest Oval East of the River

Anacostia Park's track carries the weight of decades in its surface — rubberized asphalt that absorbs heat by ten in the morning and holds it well past evening. The infield has hosted little league, political rallies, and the quiet persistence of the same group of elderly walkers who have come every Tuesday since 1987.

It is the most democratic of DC's ovals: no gate, no fee, no expectation of speed. The river is close enough that you can hear it on windless nights, and the flight paths from Reagan National measure your splits in 737 shadows.

Distance 440y / ~400m
Surface Rubberized Asphalt
Ward Ward 8
Opens Dawn — Dusk
