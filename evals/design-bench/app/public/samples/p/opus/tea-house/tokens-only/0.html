On Sourcing

Twice a year our owner, Mara, packs a small bag and travels to the gardens we pour from. In the high mist of Nantou she walks the oolong terraces with the families who roast each batch by hand; in the Nandi Hills of Kenya she sits with growers over the first picking of the season. Every leaf on the menu has a face and a place behind it.
