Where the Leaves Come From

Twice a year our owner, Mira, travels to the gardens behind every cup we pour. In Taiwan she walks the misted slopes of Alishan and Nantou, tasting fresh-rolled oolongs straight from the wok. In Kenya she meets the growers of the Nandi Hills and Rift Valley, choosing the season's brightest black teas by hand. Everything on our shelf is bought from people she has shaken hands with.
