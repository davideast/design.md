Twice a year the shop closes its doors and the owner travels to the gardens themselves — the high terraces above Chiayi, the cool slopes of the Nandi Hills. Every tea on this counter was tasted at its source, in the room where it was rolled and dried, and chosen by hand from the season's lots. Nothing arrives here that wasn't first met at the place it grew. The trips are slow and the selections are small, but that is the point: a short list, fully known, brewed the same way each morning until the next harvest comes in.
