No. 01 · Georgetown Waterfront Track

A river-edge oval that runs fast at first light.

Tucked between the towpath and the Potomac, the Waterfront oval has the quickest surface in the city — a resilient terracotta that gives a little and gives it back. Morning crews share the lanes with rowers heading down to the boathouses, and the mist off the water burns away by the third mile.

The club has timed its spring intervals here since the new resurfacing of 1998, and the back straight still holds the fastest 400 ever recorded by a member.

No. 02 · Anacostia Park Track

A patient eastside oval that rewards the regular.

East of the river, the Anacostia track keeps slower company — neighbourhood walkers, school squads, and the early-shift workers who lap it before the sun clears the treeline. The surface is older, weathered to a deeper rust, and it asks for honest effort rather than handing out fast times.

It is the club's true home: less polished than the Waterfront, but the place where most of our hundred years of mileage was actually run.
