Kettle & Leaf

Welcome to our neighborhood tea house, where each cup is brewed slowly and served with care.

Alishan Oolong

Origin: Chiayi County, Taiwan

Tasting note: orchid aroma, cream, and a clean mountain finish.

Nandi Hills Black

Origin: Nandi County, Kenya

Tasting note: bright malt, red currant, and a brisk coppery lift.

Uji Sencha

Origin: Kyoto Prefecture, Japan

Tasting note: steamed greens, sweet grass, and a soft sea breeze.

Owner Mara Lin spends each spring visiting tea gardens in Taiwan and each autumn with smallholder growers in Kenya, tasting early lots, walking the rows, and choosing leaves from producers who tend their fields with patience and respect.
