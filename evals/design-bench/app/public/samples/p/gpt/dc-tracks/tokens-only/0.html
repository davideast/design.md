Georgetown Waterfront Track

A river-edge oval with the city moving just beyond lane eight.

At dawn, the surface holds the cool air off the Potomac and gives back a clean, measured rebound. The regulars arrive quietly: rowers cooling down, office runners stealing miles, and sprinters testing the straight while the bridges brighten.

It feels less like escape than calibration, a place where Washington’s noise becomes rhythm.

Anacostia Park Track

A broad, democratic loop where effort has room to breathe.

Here the track sits low and open, near fields, trees, and the long bend of the river. Its lanes carry school workouts, neighborhood walkers, youth meets, and solitary laps taken in the heavy gold of late afternoon.

The character is generous: unhurried, durable, and deeply public.
