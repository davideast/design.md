Georgetown Waterfront Track

A measured oval beside the morning river.

At first light, the track takes on the color of brick dust and low sun, a quiet counterpoint to the traffic sliding over the water. Runners arrive singly, orbiting the same patient geometry while the city gathers itself beyond the lanes.

Its appeal is not spectacle but rhythm: the river air, the even surface, the sense that each lap has been kept for you.

Anacostia Park Track

A public lane where the city opens wide.

Here the track feels expansive, held by parkland, river light, and the long democratic promise of open space. It is a place for repeaters and walkers, school teams and solitary dawn miles, all sharing the same red curve.

The surface carries the practical grace of a civic object: durable, available, and quietly beloved by those who know its hours.
