## Overview

The publication should feel athletic, timeless, editorial, and earned. The design is serious and dignified without being stuffy: classic, authoritative, unhurried, and quietly passionate. It should read as prestige journalism about an everyday subject — elevated, refined, and sincere — with a groundedness that honors the runners and the places. The overall impression is enduring rather than trendy, documentary rather than promotional, and reverent rather than commercial. Everything about the page should communicate craft, history, and respect for the sport.

## Colors

The palette is deep, warm, and natural. **Primary** {colors.primary} is a rich, dark green for text and headings — solemn and rooted. **Neutral** {colors.neutral} is a warm, soft cream canvas, organic and calm. **Tertiary** {colors.tertiary} is an earthy, vivid accent used sparingly for moments of energy. **Secondary** {colors.secondary} is a muted, weathered tone for supporting details. Together they create a handsome, balanced, storied atmosphere.

## Typography

Typography is authoritative, elegant, and editorial. Headlines in {typography.headline-lg} are commanding and classic, never shouty. Body prose in {typography.body-md} is warm, readable, and literary. Labels in {typography.label-sm} are precise and quietly technical. Sizes are confident, weights restrained, and the hierarchy is graceful and assured.

## Layout

The layout is composed, spacious, and deliberate. Photography and text share the page in generous, balanced compositions; margins are ample, pacing is unhurried, and the whole page feels curated, premium, and complete.
