## Overview

The digital home for Kettle & Leaf must feel like a quiet, sun-drenched sanctuary that welcomes neighbors with an unhurried, artisanal warmth.

## Method

The mood is gentle, settled, and homemade—a space that feels crafted without being precious. Everything should read as calm and patient: soft, low, and quietly confident. The page is friendly but never chatty, plain but never bare, and modest but visibly cared for. It should feel light, slow, and rounded at the edges, never sharp or hurried. True warmth comes from a low golden glow, evoking a small room with a kettle on and fogged windows. The aesthetic is honest and slightly unpolished, the work of a shopkeeper who remembers every regular’s name and preference. Nothing here should feel glossy, corporate, or loud; aim instead for the soft-spoken, the handwritten, and the lived-in. The experience is restful, airy, and sincere. The daily teas are celebrated yet understated, given their own small dignity without unnecessary fuss. The owner’s sourcing stories should feel curious and well-worn but humble, like a friend sharing a discovery rather than a brand selling a lifestyle. Above all, the page should feel like an open door on a grey afternoon: clear light, an uncluttered surface, and the unforced confidence of a place that is here to stay.

## Colors

Use the warm canvas {colors.neutral} as an inviting, papery base. Set the content in the calm near-black {colors.primary} for grounded readability. Apply the quiet grey {colors.secondary} to supporting details that should recede. Introduce the single warm accent {colors.tertiary} rarely and deliberately, a touch of brewed color that rewards a second look.

## Typography

Headings in {typography.headline-lg} are graceful and assured, never showy. Running text in {typography.body-md} is comfortable and readable, made for lingering. Small labels in {typography.label-sm} are tidy and discreet, maintaining a modest and calm hierarchy.

## Layout

The page is spacious and unhurried, with generous breathing room and a balanced arrangement that walks a visitor gently from the welcome to the teas.
