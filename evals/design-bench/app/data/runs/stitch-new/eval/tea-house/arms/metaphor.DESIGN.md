## Overview

The page is a clear glass of tea held up to a window—colour suspended in light, with nothing behind it.

## Method

Steeping is the governing idea, and every element maps onto it. A glass held to the light has no dark side and casts no shadow into itself: light passes straight through, so the page is lit, not lit-from-above. It is flat warm paper {colors.neutral} with no element floating over another, because in clear water nothing hovers and nothing falls into shade. The colour in the glass is the leaf releasing into the water, and that is the one warm accent {colors.tertiary}: it belongs only to the act of brewing, so it appears where the brew is—the bloom of colour beneath the shop name, the single mark at today's date, or the first letter of each tea. It never appears structurally, because tinting the frame would mean colour where no leaf is steeping.

The three brewed teas are three steeps in a tasting flight, set side by side at equal strength in {typography.body-md} so the eye can read one against the next. The owner's sourcing paragraph is the water itself—the medium everything dissolves into—a calm clear block of prose the colour of plain ink {colors.primary}. The hours, address, and phone are sediment settling at the bottom of the glass: fine, quiet, true, ranked in {typography.label-sm} along the foot where settled things come to rest. Darkness is over-steeping—the brew gone bitter and opaque—so it has no place here; the glass stays clear, the light stays in it, and what isn't suspended in that light simply isn't on the page.

## Colors

Light through the glass: paper {colors.neutral} as the lit field, plain ink {colors.primary} as the water, settled grey {colors.secondary} for the sediment of rules and tables, and the leaf's colour {colors.tertiary} only where something is steeping.

## Typography

{typography.headline-lg} for the name, the welcome poured beneath it; {typography.body-md} for the three steeps and the water of the essay; {typography.label-sm} for the sediment—hours, address, phone—at the foot.

## Layout

A tall clear glass: an open lit field with wide margins, the three steeps ranked level across the middle, the essay as the body of water around them, and the settled credits resting along the bottom edge. The page closes with the ruled credits block.
