## Overview

Auralis should feel modern, clean, premium, and minimal. The design is technical and confident without being cold: quiet, refined, precise, and luminous. It should read as enterprise-grade craftsmanship — sophisticated, intentional, and trustworthy — with a soft approachability that keeps the precision from feeling sterile. The overall impression is airy rather than heavy, structured rather than busy, and elegant rather than flashy. Everything about the page should communicate quality, restraint, and quiet authority.

## Colors

The palette is calm, monochromatic, and assured. **Primary** {colors.primary} is a dense near-black for headlines and key actions — authoritative and grounded. **Neutral** {colors.neutral} is a warm, soft off-white canvas, gentle and paper-like. **Tertiary** {colors.tertiary} is a vivid, energetic accent that brings moments of color and life. **Secondary** {colors.secondary} is a warm mid-grey for supporting text that recedes politely. Together they create a serene, polished, high-end atmosphere.

## Typography

Typography is crisp, contemporary, and architectural. Headlines in {typography.h1} and {typography.h2} are bold and striking, tightly set. Body text in {typography.body-md} is efficient and readable. Labels in {typography.label-caps} are technical and precise, and buttons in {typography.button} are neat and purposeful. The hierarchy is immediate, confident, and clean.

## Layout

The layout is spacious, orderly, and rhythmic. Sections breathe with generous whitespace, alignment is exact, compositions feel balanced and intentional, and the page flows smoothly from statement to product to proof to action.
