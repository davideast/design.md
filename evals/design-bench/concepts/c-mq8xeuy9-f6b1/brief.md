# Brief: The Analog Stack

A music player app that feels like browsing a personal 1990s cassette collection.

## Content Sections

### 1. Header
- **Title:** "The Analog Stack"
- **Input:** A "Search the Crate" text field.

### 2. The Shelf (Library)
- A grid/list of albums.
- **Data per item:** Album Art, Artist Name, Album Title, Release Year.

### 3. Now Playing Screen
- **Track Info:** Song Title, Artist, Album Title.
- **The Tape Meter:** A progress bar visual that indicates "Side A" (0-50%) and "Side B" (51-100%).
- **Transport Controls:** Play, Pause, Rewind, Fast Forward, and "Eject" (navigation back to shelf).

### 4. The Liner Notes
- A dedicated text area for technical credits.
- **Fields:** "Produced by...", "Recorded at...", and a "Special Thanks" list.

### 5. The Mixtape View
- A list of user-selected tracks (numbered 1-10).
- **Header:** A custom editable title field (e.g., "Summer '96", "Drive Home").
