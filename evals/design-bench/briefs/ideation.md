# Brief — ideation

Design a desktop web page where a maker develops a new case in conversation with an ideation agent, before any case files exist. Two regions: the two captured artifacts (a brief and a direction), and the conversation that converges on them. This brief carries content only — all visual direction comes from the project's DESIGN.md.

The page contains:

## 1. Wayfinding and the spark

A link back to the case gallery, and the spark that started this concept, quoted: "a field notes site for night birding in the city."

## 2. The captured brief

A section titled "Captured brief," containing the agreed content document as readable prose, beginning: "Design a field-notes site for night birding in the city: a season log of nocturnal walks…" If nothing is captured yet the section instead says: "Nothing yet — converge on content in the conversation; the agent captures only when you agree."

## 3. The captured direction

A section titled "Captured direction," containing the agreed direction document: its tokens summarized in one line ("4 colors, 2 type roles, tight spacing — drawn from 1960s offset printing") and its prose beginning: "The Urban Nocturnal Field Guide is a mid-century scientific manual brought to life…"

## 4. Commit

A section titled "Commit," with one input for the case's short name (example value: "night-birding"), an action reading "Commit — project into treatments," and one sentence: "Committing scaffolds the case from the captured direction and writes all treatments from it; it is available once both artifacts are captured, and it is yours — the agent never commits."

## 5. The conversation

A persistent panel showing the ideation exchange. It contains: a capture-state line ("brief ✓ · direction ✓ — ready to commit"), three turns of real conversation — maker: "night birding in the city" / agent: "The default here is a sleek dark-mode dashboard with neon accents and owl photography. Three worlds that escape it: a 1960s Peterson field guide; a transit-system night map; a pirate-radio log zine." / maker: "the field guide — capture both" / agent: "Captured the brief (24 lines) and the direction (34 lines). You can commit when ready." — an input area with the placeholder "React, redirect, choose…" and quick actions: "3 worlds," "name the enemy," "directions," "capture brief," "capture direction."
