How to read the evidence

  • Treatment: The specific linguistic framing applied to the prompt (e.g., specifying an object vs. a mood).
  • Default Look: The baseline stylistic output of the model without specific grounding constraints.
  • Adjectives: The standard industry approach of stacking descriptive words to steer aesthetic outcomes.
