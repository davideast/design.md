## Overview
This design for Pocket Tides establishes a high-contrast, technical interface tailored for the rugged conditions of Ocean Beach. It prioritizes immediate legibility and a functional, maritime aesthetic that feels like a professional tool rather than a consumer weather app.

## Method
The visual language is rooted in industrial utility and nautical precision. The interface uses a stark, high-contrast approach to ensure visibility under the harsh, flat light of a foggy coast or the direct glare of the sun. The aesthetic is "instrument-grade," avoiding any decorative flourishes in favor of a raw, systematic presentation of data. Every element is treated as a vital piece of information, presented with a clarity that suggests a mission-critical maritime device.

## Colors
The palette uses {colors.primary} as the dominant ink, creating a heavy, grounded feel against the crisp {colors.neutral} canvas. {colors.secondary} is utilized for secondary data points and supporting structural elements, providing a clear hierarchy without sacrificing the starkness of the layout. {colors.tertiary} is reserved exclusively for critical tide alerts and the "Paddle Out" recommendation, acting as a high-visibility safety signal that cuts through the cooler tones.

## Typography
The typography is built for rapid data ingestion. {typography.headline-lg} is used for the location and primary tide heights, appearing in a bold, condensed format that suggests a technical manual or an equipment label. {typography.body-md} handles the surf utility notes with clean, unadorned clarity. {typography.label-sm} is applied to all timestamps and environmental data, using its monospaced character to evoke the feel of a printed tide table or a digital sensor readout.

## Layout
The layout is a rigid, modular grid that maximizes screen real estate. Information is packed into dense, clearly defined blocks. The tide visualization is a sharp, technical curve that spans the width of the screen, while environmental data is organized in a tight, multi-column format. The overall structure is flat and uncompromising, emphasizing a sense of reliability and technical rigor.
