## Overview

This page embodies a single governing idea.

## Method

The oval is a monument, and the page is a lap of it. Every city has one — worn terracotta, chalked lanes, the same quarter mile run ten thousand times by ten thousand different people — and a monument is approached with reverence, not marketing. So the reader does not browse this page; they run it. Each full-viewport panel is a stretch of the circuit: the hero is the start line, the track portraits are the straights where the eye settles into rhythm, the pull quote is the back straight where there is nothing to do but think, the map is the aerial view a runner holds in their head, and the sign-up is the final bend that delivers you back where you began, breathing harder. Scrolling is pacing: deliberate, metered, one stride per panel, never hurried by animation or interrupted by furniture. The lane markings run onto the page as thin ruled lines — horizontal at full width between panels, vertical within them — low-contrast and architectural, the chalk geometry that tells a runner where they are without ever being looked at. The monument's materials set the register: infield green {colors.primary} for words, programme cream {colors.neutral} underfoot, weathered slate {colors.secondary} for the small inscriptions, and one band of terracotta {colors.tertiary} per panel — the start line crossed once each lap, the only thing at a track that ever shouts. Monuments are inscribed, not decorated: headlines carved in the editorial serif {typography.headline-lg}, the catalogue's patient prose in {typography.body-md}, and every distance, surface, and split chiseled in the timekeeper's monospace {typography.label-sm}. Nothing on a monument glows, floats, or rounds; what endures is flat, exact, and earned.

## Colors

The monument's materials: green {colors.primary}, cream {colors.neutral}, slate {colors.secondary}, and the start-line terracotta {colors.tertiary}, once per lap.

## Typography

Inscription headlines in {typography.headline-lg}, guidebook prose in {typography.body-md}, chiseled data in {typography.label-sm}.

## Layout

One lap: full-viewport panels in sequence, lane-marking rules between and within them, deliberate scroll pacing, the final panel returning the reader to the start.
