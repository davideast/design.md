From Garden to Kettle

Our journey begins in the mist-covered hills of Taiwan and the sun-drenched gardens of Kenya. Every leaf is hand-selected during the owner's annual sourcing trips, where we partner directly with small-batch farmers who share our commitment to soil health and traditional craftsmanship. We bring these stories back to your cup.
