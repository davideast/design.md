Georgetown Waterfront Track

A river oval with the city moving at its shoulder.

Here the morning arrives over the Potomac in long bands of slate and gold, and every lap seems to borrow its rhythm from the water. The surface is firm, honest, and quick enough for intervals, but the place rewards looking up: bridges, rowers, commuters, the whole city passing just beyond lane eight.

Its regulars come early, before the waterfront fills, measuring the same curve against a changing sky.

Anacostia Park Track

A broad, democratic loop beside the river’s eastern light.

Anacostia Park carries its track with the calm authority of public ground: open sky, deep green edges, and the steady sound of shoes finding tempo. The oval feels generous rather than grand, a place for school teams, solo miles, recovery walks, and the old ritual of starting again.

In the heat, the terracotta lanes hold the day; at dusk, they cool into a quiet circuit.
