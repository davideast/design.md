Georgetown Waterfront Track

A river oval with morning light on the rail.

The Georgetown circuit feels borrowed from the water: open, bright, and wind-marked, with the Potomac keeping time beside the back straight. Runners arrive before the offices wake, trading quiet laps for the hour when the city is still mostly stone, gulls, and breath.

Underfoot, the surface is forgiving without feeling soft, a measured place for tempo work and the small rituals of returning.

Anacostia Park Track

A broad eastside course, plainspoken and exact.

Anacostia Park’s track carries the generous scale of the fields around it: sky above the turns, trees beyond the fence, and enough room for a club workout to unfold without ceremony. It is a democratic oval, used hard and known locally by the sound of spikes, school practices, and evening repeats.

The lane lines hold their promise: come back tomorrow, measure again, begin at the same mark.
