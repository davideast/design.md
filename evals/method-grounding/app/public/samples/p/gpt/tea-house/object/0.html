Sourcing Notes

Owner Mara Lin travels each spring to Taiwan’s high mountain gardens and each autumn to Kenya’s central highlands, buying from growers who pluck by hand, rest the leaf carefully, and know every slope by weather and soil. Those trips shape the cups served here: teas chosen for clarity, freshness, and the stories carried from farm gate to kettle.
