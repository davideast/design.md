Georgetown Waterfront Track

Where the river sets the pace.

Tucked beneath the rowing crews and the morning haze of the Potomac, this is a track that runs against the current. Its terracotta lanes hold the cool of the water long after sunrise, and the regulars know the back straight catches the wind first. Few ovals in the city feel so much like a balcony over the river.

Anacostia Park Track

A wide oval for a wide sky.

East of the river, the city opens up. Anacostia's track is broad, unhurried, and loud with neighborhood — children on the infield, elders walking lane eight, sprinters tuning up at dusk. It is less a venue than a commons, an oval that belongs to everyone who shows up.

SurfaceRubberized
