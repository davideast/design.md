Guide to Listening — The Urban Filter

To identify birds by sound in the city, you must mentally subtract the machine:

  1. The BaseIgnore the low-frequency rumble of the expressway or subway vents.
  2. The SignalFocus on the "Peent"—a sharp, nasal electric buzz cutting through the hum.
  3. The PhysicalListen for the "Whoosh" of air through primary feathers during a territorial dive.
