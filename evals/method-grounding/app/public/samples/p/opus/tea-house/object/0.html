From the owner

Every spring I close the shop for three weeks and go to the source. In the cool hills above Nantou I sit with the same family I have bought from for nine years, drinking the season's first oolong as it comes off the roaster, choosing by the cup rather than the catalogue.

Later in the year the trip turns to the Rift Valley in Kenya, where smallholder gardens grow the strange purple-leaf cultivars almost no one else carries. I bring back only what I would happily pour for a friend — which is to say, everything you find steeping here.
