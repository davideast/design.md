Our tea is the result of long-distance relationships. Every spring, we travel from the high-elevation gardens of Nantou, Taiwan, to the volcanic red soils of Mt. Kenya. We source directly from smallholder farmers who prioritize soil health and traditional processing. These trips allow us to select specific lots that reflect the weather and character of each season, ensuring every cup poured in our shop tells the honest story of the land it came from.
