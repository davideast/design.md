Our Sourcing

Our tea is born from quiet journeys. I travel annually to the mist-shrouded slopes of Taiwan and the high-altitude gardens of Kenya to source leaves directly from families who have tended the same soil for generations. These trips aren't just about the harvest; they're about the slow conversations and shared pots that define the spirit of Kettle & Leaf.
