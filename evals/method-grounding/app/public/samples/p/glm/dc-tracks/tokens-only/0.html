Georgetown Waterfront Track

A flat oval pressed against the Potomac, where the river sets the pace.

Morning rowers drift past while early crews turn intervals under the low sun. The surface is forgiving, the wind off the water constant, and the view westward — toward Rosslyn's glass — is the closest thing the city has to a horizon. You come here to feel fast without pretending you are.

Plate 02 · Anacostia
Track 02 of Six
Anacostia Park Track

A wide, weathered oval hidden inside a corridor of cottonwoods.

Anacostia's track is older, softer, quieter. High-school teams share lanes with retirees walking dogs; the surface has the give of something that has forgiven ten thousand footfalls. To run here is to feel the city's east, its slower rhythm, its longer memory.
