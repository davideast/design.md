Georgetown Waterfront Track

Where the river keeps the time, and the oarsmen set the pace.

Tucked beneath the Key Bridge and the watchful stroke of eights from the Potomac Boat Club, this four-lane oval is the quietest quarter-mile in the District. The surface is forgiving; the view, less so — you will run slower than you intended, and you will not mind.

Pl. 02 — Anacostia Park
No. 02 / 06
Anacostia Park Track

A wide, windswept oval where the city finally exhales.

East of the river, the track opens onto a mile of waterfront most Washingtonians never see. Eight lanes of polyurethane, a steady off-river breeze, and the long, unbroken sightlines that make tempo runs feel like meditation. This is where the local clubs come to suffer, kindly.
