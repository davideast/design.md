§ 04 Guide to Listening — The Urban Filter

To identify birds by sound in the city, you must mentally subtract the machine. Three sequential filters isolate the signal from the urban baseline.

1
The Base

Ignore the low-frequency rumble of the expressway or subway vents.

2
The Signal

Focus on the "Peent"—a sharp, nasal electric buzz cutting through the hum.

3
The Physical

Listen for the "Whoosh" of air through primary feathers during a territorial dive.
